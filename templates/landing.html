What it does

AI

Smart triage

A text model reads your message and classifies the emergency type and severity in real time, backed by safety keyword rules.

+

First-aid guidance

Step-by-step instructions for medical, fire, accident, security and natural-disaster situations, sourced from Red Cross and NDMA guidance.

⌖

Live location

Your location is shared with campus security the moment you send an SOS, and keeps updating while the page stays open.

☎

One-tap contacts

Campus security, medical room, warden and national helplines, shown automatically for the detected emergency type.

▦

Security dashboard

Live incident feed with map, severity sorting, status tracking and exportable reports for the response team.

⚿

Private by design

Only you and campus security can see a report. Other students never see your incident or location.
